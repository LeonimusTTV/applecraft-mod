# AppleCraft

**Listen to Apple Music without leaving Minecraft.** Search for songs, open your playlists, see the artwork, and control playback from an in-game screen.

## Features

- 🎵 **Full Apple Music catalog**: search songs, albums, artists and playlists
- 📚 **Your library**: recently played and your own playlists
- 🖼️ **Artwork everywhere**: in the browser screen, the player bar and the HUD
- ⏯️ **Player bar**: play/pause, next/previous, shuffle, repeat, seek, volume
- 🪧 **"Now playing" card** in the HUD, in the corner of your choice (can be turned off)
- ⌨️ **Hotkeys** to control music while playing
- 🌍 English and French

## How to use

1. Install the mod (and **Fabric API** if you use Fabric).
2. Launch the game: an **AppleCraft tab opens in your browser**. Keep it open, it's the one playing your music.
3. In that tab, **sign in to Apple Music** and click the **▶ button** once to allow sound (browsers block sound until you click).
4. In game, press **N** to open AppleCraft.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **N** | Open / close AppleCraft |
| **K** | Play / pause |
| **→ / ←** | Next / previous track |

All keys can be changed in *Options → Controls → AppleCraft*.

## Requirements

- An **Apple Music subscription** for full tracks. Without signing in, search works but you only get 30-second previews.
- A web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge…).
- Client-side only: not needed on servers, works in singleplayer and multiplayer.

## FAQ

**Why does it open a browser tab?**
Apple Music streams are protected, and only Apple's official web player (MusicKit) can play them. The mod runs that player in a tab and controls it from the game.

**I hear nothing.**
Click the **▶ button** in the AppleCraft tab (its title blinks when it's waiting for you). You only need to do it once while the tab stays open.

**Is my Apple account safe?**
You sign in on Apple's own page. The mod never sees your password, and everything runs on your computer: the game talks to the tab through `localhost` only.

**Fabric or NeoForge?**
Both, on Minecraft 26.3. More versions are planned.
